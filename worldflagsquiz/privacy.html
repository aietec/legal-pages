Privacy Policy

Application: World Flags Quiz • November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how World Flags Quiz (the "App"), published by AIETEC ("we", "us"), handles information. The App works on your device and does not require an account.

1. Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or location. We operate no server that receives your answers, scores or progress.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores locally, in its private storage: your scores, stars per level, best streaks, conquered countries on the map, and your settings (sound, haptics). This data stays on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the App or use Settings → Reset Progress.

3. Permissions

The App uses the Internet and network-state permissions to display ads, and the vibration permission for optional haptic feedback. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (banner and interstitial ads). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process, through the App, information such as your device identifiers (including the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and interactions with ads. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive this data and cannot identify you from it.

5. Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and similar regions: the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads that use personal data are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy settings (ads) in the App.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

6. Screen casting

The optional cast shortcut opens your device's own casting settings. The App does not collect any information about your casting devices.

7. Children

The App is a general-audience educational quiz and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age applicable in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EEA, the UK or another region with data protection laws, you have rights regarding data processed by Google; you can exercise them with Google, or contact us at contact@aietec.net and we will help where we can.

9. Security and retention

Local data stays in the App's private storage on your device. We keep no data about you on our side, so there is no retention period to apply.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add features or change providers. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
