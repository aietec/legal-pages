Privacy Policy

Application: AssistBox • Updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AssistBox (the "App"), published by AIETEC ("we", "us"), handles information. The App is designed to process your content on your device and to collect as little as possible.

1. Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or location. We operate no server that receives your documents, images, text or recordings. The App does not upload them to us.

2. Permissions and how they are used

  • Camera: to scan documents and take photos of pages. Images are processed on your device.
  • Microphone: to record voice memos and for dictation. Audio is recorded and transcribed on your device.
  • Photos, media and audio files: only when you choose to import an image or an audio file to scan, extract text from, or transcribe.
  • Bluetooth: to use a Bluetooth headset or microphone for recording.
  • Internet: used for advertising and for the in-app purchase (see sections 4 and 5).

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your Android settings. Some features then stop working.

3. Data processed and stored on your device

Scanning, text recognition (OCR, using Google ML Kit and Tesseract), speech-to-text (Whisper) and text-to-speech run on your device. The recognition models are included in the App and are not downloaded.

The App stores locally: your scan history (up to the 30 most recent documents), your voice recordings, files you choose to save, and your settings (language, voice options, ad-consent choice). This data stays on your device unless you decide to share or export it, for example to another app. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage, and you can delete scans and recordings inside the App at any time.

Text-to-speech uses the speech engine installed on your device. Depending on your device manufacturer, that engine is provided by a third party (for example Google) and is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (banner and interstitial ads). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process, through the App, information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This processing is performed by Google under its own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

5. In-app purchase (RevenueCat)

A one-time in-app purchase can remove ads. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details. To manage and verify the purchase, the App uses RevenueCat, which processes an anonymous app user identifier, purchase information and basic device information, under its own policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

6. Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and similar regions: the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads that use personal data are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the App's privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (the exact path depends on your device).
  • If you do not consent, ads may still be shown but are limited to non-personalized ads where the law requires it.

7. Children

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age applicable in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EEA, the UK or another region with data protection laws, you have rights regarding data processed by Google and RevenueCat; you can exercise them with those companies, or contact us at contact@aietec.net and we will help where we can.

9. Security and retention

Local data stays in the App's private storage on your device. We keep no data about you on our side, so there is no retention period to apply. Files you share or export leave the App and are then under your control and that of the receiving app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add features or change providers. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
