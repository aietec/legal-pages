Support

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Contact

Write to Information to complete. We do our best to reply as soon as practicable on business days; no response time is guaranteed.

Useful details

Include platform, OS version, Magnivio version, problem description and reproduction steps. If needed and available through diagnostics, include the anonymous RevenueCat identifier.

Never send

Never send a password, card number, security code, API key or a full receipt containing sensitive information.

Restore purchases

Open Premium and choose Restore purchases while signed into the same store account. Confirm network access and restart the app if needed.

Subscription not recognized

Check the store subscription, account and connection, then restore. Billing/refunds are handled by Google Play or Apple; Support can help diagnose RevenueCat entitlement synchronization.

Reward not credited

A reward needs network access, a completed ad, a valid signed SSV callback and RevenueCat confirmation. Wait briefly and refresh Premium. The offer is once per user and an ad may be unavailable.

Camera permissions

Allow Camera in system settings. Torch and some controls depend on device capabilities. Restart after changing permission if necessary.

Local images

Images are in Magnivio’s private gallery. Export them through Share before uninstalling or clearing app data. Deleted local images may not be recoverable.

Privacy requests

Contact Information to complete. State your request and jurisdiction; provide the anonymous RevenueCat identifier only if needed. Do not send sensitive payment data.
