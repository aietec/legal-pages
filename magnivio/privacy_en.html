Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. Who controls your data

The publisher of Magnivio is the data controller: Information to complete. Contact: Information to complete.

2. Camera and images

Magnivio requests camera access solely to provide live magnification, zoom, focus, torch, frozen-image, brightness and negative-filter features. The camera is not required hardware on Android, so availability depends on the device.

When you choose to save a capture, Magnivio re-encodes it without EXIF metadata and stores the image, thumbnail and local index in the app’s private support directory. The private gallery lists these local files. You can delete one or all images. Sharing occurs only after your action through the device share sheet. Magnivio does not upload these images to its servers. Uninstalling the app will normally delete its private files, subject to the operating system’s backup and restoration behavior; export important images first.

3. Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Monthly and annual Premium purchases are processed by Google Play, or by Apple App Store when a commercial iOS version is available. RevenueCat, Inc. technically validates purchases and manages Premium access. It may process a RevenueCat app user identifier (normally an SDK-generated anonymous identifier), purchase history and status, store receipts or Google purchase tokens, product, purchase/renewal/expiry dates, entitlement status, platform and service diagnostics. RevenueCat does not directly process the payment-card details entered in the store. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Optional rewarded advertising

You may voluntarily request a Google AdMob rewarded ad. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) collects and applies available consent/privacy choices before ads are requested. Depending on your choices, configuration and platform, Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device and interaction data, approximate location, consent signals and ad diagnostics. Non-personalized ads can still use identifiers for frequency capping and aggregate reporting. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses advertising data.

Before showing the ad, Magnivio supplies the RevenueCat app user identifier as SSV custom data. AdMob sends a cryptographically signed server-side verification callback containing the ad transaction information. After successful server validation, Premium may be granted for exactly 72 hours. The current policy permits one rewarded grant per RevenueCat user.

5. Firebase and server records

The rewarded-access backend uses Firebase Cloud Functions and Firestore. It stores hashed representations of the AdMob transaction ID and RevenueCat identifier, the ad-unit/reward values needed for validation, processing status, source, server timestamps, grant expiry and a short technical error code when applicable. Raw identifiers are not used as document names. These records provide idempotence, prevent duplicate grants, diagnose failures and prove entitlement attribution. Client access to these collections is denied.

6. Purposes, legal bases and recipients

Local image processing is necessary to provide the feature you request. Subscription processing is necessary to perform the purchase contract. Security, fraud prevention, diagnostics and entitlement records rely on contract performance and the publisher’s legitimate interests. Advertising storage/access and personalization rely on consent where law requires it; you can revisit advertising privacy choices in the app when UMP makes that option available.

Recipients are the publisher and, as applicable, RevenueCat, Google/AdMob/Firebase, Google Play and Apple. The static website itself sets no cookie and uses no analytics, although GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security.

7. Retention, security and international transfers

Images remain until you delete them or the app’s private data is removed. Store and subscription data follow the retention rules of the stores and RevenueCat. Reward-validation records are retained only as long as needed for attribution, fraud prevention, support and legal obligations; a precise production period must be documented by the publisher. Providers may process data outside the EEA, including in the United States, using their published transfer safeguards.

Reasonable measures include private on-device storage, removal of EXIF metadata, encrypted transport, signed SSV callbacks, hashed record keys, server-only Firestore access and least-privilege secrets. No system is completely secure.

8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing, and withdraw consent where consent is the basis. EU users may complain to their supervisory authority; in France, to the CNIL. Contact Information to complete.

Magnivio has no account or email collection. To help locate subscription or reward records, Support may ask for your anonymous RevenueCat identifier if it can be displayed or retrieved through a realistic diagnostic procedure. Do not send payment credentials or a full sensitive receipt. Anonymous or hashed records may be impossible to link to you without that identifier, and legally required anti-fraud/accounting records may not be immediately deletable.

9. Changes

This policy is effective on 11 September 2026. Material changes will be posted here with a new date and, where appropriate, communicated in the app. Review this page periodically.
