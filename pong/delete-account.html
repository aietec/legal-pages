CRT PONG - 1972 RETRO ARCADE

Account & Data Deletion

Google Play Compliance & Privacy Protection

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and global privacy regulations, users of CRT Pong - 1972 Retro Arcade ("the Application"), published by AieTec, can request the complete deletion of their account and associated data.

Deletion Procedure

Follow these steps to unlink or permanently delete your account data:

Step 1: In-App Sign Out or Unlink

Users can unlink their Google account or sign out directly within the application settings at any time.

Step 2: Request Permanent Data Deletion

To request permanent deletion of your account data, Firebase profile, leaderboard scores, and matchmaking history, simply send an email request to our support address:

To: contact@aietec.net
Subject: Delete Account - CRT Pong
Details Required: Specify your Google account email or in-game User ID.

Processing Timeline & Data Affected

  • Timeline: Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days.
  • Data Deleted: Your Firebase profile, registered email address, global leaderboard scores, ELO rating, and matchmaking history will be permanently erased.

Contact Support

If you have any questions regarding your account or data deletion request, please reach out to us at contact@aietec.net.
