2. Information We Collect

To provide multiplayer arcade gameplay, global leaderboards, ad-supported access, and optional purchases, the Application collects the following categories of data:

  • Authentication (Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication): When you log in, we collect your Firebase unique User ID (UID), public display name, and technical email address provided by Google Sign-In. No password storage is performed.
  • Gameplay Data: Global leaderboard scores, ELO rating, matchmaking metadata, and match history associated with your account.
  • Monetization & Ads (Google AdMob): The Application integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob collects device identifiers, mobile advertising IDs, and technical specifications for ad delivery and analytics. We utilize Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent management framework to collect and manage user consent preferences for GDPR and CCPA compliance.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing): Purchase tokens and transaction identifiers issued by Google Play Billing when unlocking in-app content or VIP status. Payments are securely processed by Google Play.

3. Data Usage & Real-Time Services

Collected data is used strictly for core application functionality and services:

  • WebRTC Peer Connection Setup: Signaling metadata exchanged during matchmaking to establish peer-to-peer real-time multiplayer connections.
  • Matchmaking Lobbies & Leaderboards: Matchmaking lobby management, calculating global ELO ratings, and ranking top scores on global leaderboards.
  • Support & Receipt Verification: Validating in-app purchases and providing technical assistance upon user request.

4. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted between the Application and our cloud services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. User profiles, gameplay metadata, and leaderboard scores are stored securely within Google Firebase infrastructure equipped with robust security rules and administrative controls.

5. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under GDPR, CCPA, and applicable data privacy regulations, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Right to request details and a copy of the personal data processed.
  • Right to Rectify: Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete profile data.
  • Right to Delete: Right to request permanent deletion of your account and associated data.
