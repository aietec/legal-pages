Privacy Policy

Application: Foldori • Updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Foldori (the "App"), published by AIETEC ("we", "us"), handles information. We designed the App to collect as little as possible.

1. Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We operate no server that receives data from the App.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your device only: your best score, your game settings (such as showing level numbers), the state of your current game, and your ad-consent choice. This data never leaves your device through us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process, through the App, information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This processing is performed by Google under its own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game helper.

4. Consent and your choices

  • EEA, UK and similar regions: the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads that use personal data are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices (gear icon next to the score).
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, the exact path depends on your device).
  • If you do not consent, ads may still be shown but will be limited to non-personalized ads where the law requires it.

5. Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age applicable in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in the EEA, the UK or another region with data protection laws, you have rights regarding data processed by Google for advertising; you can exercise them with Google, or contact us at contact@aietec.net and we will help where we can.

7. Security and retention

Local data stays in the App's private storage on your device. We keep no data about you on our side, so there is no retention period to apply.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add features or change ad providers. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
