This Privacy Policy explains how AieTec ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you play Breakout — Neo-Retro Arcade Edition ("the Application"). We are committed to protecting your privacy in full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) standards.

1. Data Collected

The Application is designed with a privacy-first approach and works 100% offline. No mandatory personal or nominative information (such as your real name, physical address, or phone number) is required to play.

  • No Mandatory Personal Identification: You can play both the 72-level Campaign and the 1976 Classic Mode without creating a personal account or submitting personally identifiable information.
  • Local & Technical Gameplay Data: Your high scores, unlocked worlds/levels, star ratings, language preference (EN/FR/ES/PT), audio settings, and accessibility preferences (high contrast, haptics) are stored locally on your device (`SharedPreferences`).
  • Anonymous Advertising Identifiers: When advertisements are enabled, Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may collect anonymous mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / IDFA), coarse IP-based region, device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics for frequency capping, fraud prevention, and ad delivery.

2. Third-Party Services

We integrate the following trusted third-party SDKs to support free-to-play access and optional cloud synchronization:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & User Messaging Platform (UMP): Used to display capped interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded video ads ("Continue +1 Life"). Before loading ads in regulated regions (EEA, UK, Switzerland, US states), the Google UMP SDK presents a certified consent form allowing you to accept, customize, or refuse personalized advertising.
  • Google Firebase (Optional Leaderboards & Sync): Used only if optional score synchronization is active to queue and sync anonymous high-score records when a network connection becomes available.

3. In-App Purchases (IAP)

The Application offers an optional "Remove Ads" purchase. All financial transactions are processed directly and securely by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. AieTec never accesses, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, bank details, or billing address. Only a non-personal cryptographic purchase status boolean is stored locally on your device to disable interstitials and unlock instant second-chance revives.

4. User Rights & Consent Management (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR in the European Economic Area/UK and CCPA/CPRA in California), you hold the following rights:

  • Right to Withdraw or Modify Ad Consent: You can review, change, or revoke your advertising and cookie consent choices at any time directly inside the game via Settings → Legal & Privacy → Privacy Choices (GDPR / Cookies).
  • Right of Access, Rectification, and Erasure: Because gameplay progress is stored locally on your device, you can erase all local data at any time by clearing the Application storage or uninstalling the Application. For any cloud-synced leaderboard entry, you may contact us to request immediate deletion.
  • Device-Level Opt-Out: You may also reset or delete your mobile Advertising ID in your Android (Settings → Google → Ads) or iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) system settings.

100% Offline Playability Guarantee

Refusing personalized ads or playing without an internet connection never blocks access to the game. Breakout remains fully playable offline at all times.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Non-personalized or age-appropriate ad requests are enforced where applicable under COPPA and GDPR-K regulations.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technical or regulatory changes. The updated version will always be published at this URL with its effective revision date.
