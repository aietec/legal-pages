Privacy-First Design: Board Studio is designed as an offline-first creative slate. All drawings, sketches, and notes are processed and stored exclusively on your device. We do not transmit, analyze, or host any of your artwork on remote servers.

Managing Ad & Privacy Consent in Board Studio

If you are using the ad-supported version of Board Studio in jurisdictions subject to GDPR/CCPA regulations, you can review or modify your consent preferences at any time:

  1. Open Board Studio on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Shield icon (🛡️) in the top-right corner of the canvas.
  3. Select "Ad Consent Settings (UMP)" to adjust your advertising preferences.
