Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026 • Application: Board Studio (com.aietec.blackboard)

1. Introduction

At AIETEC ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of every user of Board Studio: Draw & Sketch ("the Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates, what information is processed, and our strict safeguards regarding personal privacy.

Summary of Core Principles:
  • Board Studio is completely functional offline without creating any account.
  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your drawings, handwritten notes, or images.
  • Drawings remain 100% on your device storage unless you choose to export or share them yourself.
  • Children's privacy is protected: no behavioral profiling, registration, or data collection.

2. Data Collection and Usage

A. User Artwork, Sketches, and Local Storage

All creative content produced within Board Studio—including canvas strokes, text annotations, coloring pages, undo histories, and exported image files—is processed and saved strictly locally on your device. We do not operate remote servers to host or view user drawings.

When you grant image saving permissions or choose to save your drawing to the device gallery, the file is saved directly to your local media store using standard Android MediaStore / Storage APIs.

B. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Board Studio offers optional in-app purchases (such as the VIP All-Access Pass, Remove Ads, and Theme Coloring Packs). All payment transactions and subscriptions/in-app purchases are handled directly and securely by Google Play Billing services.

AIETEC never has access to, nor stores, your credit card details, bank information, or billing address. We only receive anonymized transaction tokens from Google Play to verify your entitlement status (e.g., whether ads should be permanently deactivated).

C. Advertising and Analytics (Free Tier Only)

In the free version of Board Studio, non-invasive advertisements may be served via the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). These may include banner, interstitial, app open, or rewarded video ads.

In jurisdictions subject to privacy regulations such as the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the UK Data Protection Act, we implement Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. This consent management platform prompts users for their explicit preferences regarding personalized vs. non-personalized advertising and device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID).

You may modify your advertising consent choices at any time from within the application by tapping the Shield icon (🛡️) on the canvas and selecting "Ad Consent Settings".

Purchasing the VIP All-Access Pass or Remove Ads feature permanently removes all advertising components and disables ad network communication.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Board Studio features an interactive Coloring Book for Kids with kid-friendly vector outlines (Animals, Space, Dinosaurs). We take children's privacy with paramount seriousness:

  • We do not collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in the European Union).
  • The coloring studio does not require any account creation or profile setup.
  • Access to the VIP Store and in-app purchases is guarded by a Parental Gate (requiring parental arithmetic verification before opening payment screens).

4. Device Permissions

Board Studio requests minimal Android permissions strictly required for core functionality:

  • INTERNET: Required for Google Play Billing validation and AdMob ad requests (free tier only). Drawing tools operate fully without an internet connection.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used only on legacy Android versions (Android 9.0 and below) to save drawings to the user gallery. On modern Android versions (Android 10+), scoped storage / MediaStore APIs are utilized without requiring broad storage permissions.

5. Third-Party Services

The application integrates the following industry-standard third-party SDKs provided by Google:

  • Google Play Services: Core platform support and update management. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Secure payment processing. (Google Play Terms)
  • Google AdMob & UMP: Privacy-compliant ad mediation and consent gathering. (Google Advertising Policies)

6. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA & Global Rights)

Depending on your location, you hold legal rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, rectify, or delete any data collected by third-party ad networks, and the right to withdraw advertising consent.

Because Board Studio does not operate user accounts or store any personal data on proprietary servers, there is no remote profile to delete. Clearing the application's local storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all locally stored data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our features, Google Play policies, or legal requirements. Updates will be published on this portal with a revised "Last updated" date.
